`@ui-sdk/solid` gives you components such as Select, Dialog, and Tabs, built on ui-sdk's behaviors. They handle interactions, focus, and ARIA attributes. You choose their markup and styles.

Import the parts you need, put them together in your app, and style them with your own CSS. The examples use Tailwind CSS.

## Explore the Components

Browse [Components](./components) to find what you need. Each page shows the component's parts, examples of what you can do with it, and its API.

The [Using Components guides](./parts-and-composition) cover patterns the components share, such as composing parts, handling state, and styling.

For a component of your own, start with [Build a Component](../core/build-a-component), then [render its parts in Solid](./rendering).
